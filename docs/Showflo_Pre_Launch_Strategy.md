# Showflo – Pre-Launch Strategy

> **Goal:** Build an audience before the product launches so Showflo already has early users, feedback, and credibility.

---

# 🚀 Core Strategy

Don't spend months building in silence.

Instead:

```
Build in Public
        ↓
Grow Audience
        ↓
Collect Waitlist
        ↓
Talk to Users
        ↓
Launch with Demand
```

The objective is simple:

- Build trust
- Build an audience
- Validate the product
- Collect early adopters
- Launch with users already waiting

# 🎯 Success Metrics Before Building the MVP

You should aim to achieve:

- ✅ 100+ waitlist signups
- ✅ 20+ broker interviews
- ✅ 500+ website visitors
- ✅ 10+ LinkedIn posts
- ✅ 4+ blog articles
- ✅ Clear product roadmap
- ✅ Community following your journey

If you achieve these milestones before the MVP is ready, you'll launch with an audience instead of starting from zero.

---

# 💡 Final Principle

Don't optimize for writing code.

Optimize for learning.

The goal is not to launch a product.

The goal is to launch a product that people are already waiting for.
