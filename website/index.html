The status quo

Today's sales teams operate in the dark.

The deck goes out, then goes silent.

A proposal is shared. Then nothing — no read receipt, no signal, no way to know if it landed or disappeared into an inbox.

Momentum is a guess, not a measurement.

Reps forecast on gut feel and a gap between calls, not on what the buyer actually did with what they were sent.

Follow-ups are generic, because context is gone.

By the time a rep circles back, the specific moment of buyer interest — the slide they lingered on — is already lost.

The shift

How showflo changes that.

showflo replaces the static deck with a living Digital Sales Room. Every open, replay, and pause becomes a signal — read in real time, turned into next steps, and shared with your whole team, without anyone having to ask the buyer what they thought.
