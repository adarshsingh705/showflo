Privacy

Last updated August 2026

Your deal data belongs to you. This page explains what we collect, why we collect it, and what we will never do with it.

What we collect

  • Account details you provide — name, work email, and company.
  • Engagement signals inside a showflo room — opens, replays, time spent, and scroll depth.
  • Content you upload to share with buyers, such as decks and proposals.

How we use it

  • To run showflo and keep your rooms and signals accurate.
  • To reach out about your account, your early access status, or product updates.
  • To detect abuse and keep the platform secure.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data.
  • We never share deal content with third-party advertisers.
  • We never use your rooms or documents to train models outside your account.

Early Access

While showflo is in early access, information you submit through Request Early Access is used only to reach you about onboarding. A complete Privacy Policy publishes at general availability and will govern all accounts going forward.
